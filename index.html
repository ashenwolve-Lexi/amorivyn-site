Features

Stories shaped by you

Everything you need to imagine, write, and play out fictional worlds with AI characters.

Create characters

Bring your own fictional characters to life and start conversations with them whenever inspiration strikes.

Shape your persona

Decide who you are in the story. Set up your persona and step into every scene your way.

Solo, group & adventure

Have one-on-one conversations, join group stories with several characters, or set out on illustrated adventures.

Character voices

Listen to supported character voices, so replies can be heard as well as read.

Make it yours

Customize how your conversations look, from dialogue fonts and colors to backgrounds and chat bubbles.

Your language, your chats

Choose the language for AI replies, and export your chats to keep the stories you've written.

How it works

Begin in three steps

  1. Get the app

    Download Amorivyn from Google Play on your Android device and sign in with Google or email.

  2. Pick or create a character

    Browse solo, group and adventure stories, or create your own character and shape your persona.

  3. Start your story

    Chat, listen to character voices, and style the conversation until it feels like your own world.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is Amorivyn for?

Amorivyn is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. Please don't use the app if you are under 18.

Where can I get Amorivyn?

Amorivyn is an Android app available on Google Play.

Are the characters real?

No. AI characters and replies are fictional and may be inaccurate. Features and availability may change while the app is in beta.

How is my data handled?

You can read exactly what is collected and how it is used in our Privacy Policy. Data is encrypted in transit, and you can request that your data be deleted.

How do I contact you?

Email us any time at Amorivyn@gmail.com.
